## Caveats
- Figure 1 (Flourish 24356715, https://flo.uri.sh/visualisation/24356715/embed?auto=1) $M: ACA expansion: work req 325,610 + provider taxes 101,810 + redeterminations 62,530 + other 35,644 = 525,594; all states: SDP 149,424 + eligibility rule 121,863 + provider taxes 89,308 + other 24,822 = 385,417; total 911,011. Mapping to CBO: expansion other = 71110 (28,200) + 71120 (7,444); eligibility rule = 71101+71102; all-states other (24,822) = remaining Medicaid sections (103,478 gross) less interactions 78,656.
- CBO Medicaid chapter (71101-71121 outlays) sums to -989,667 (KFF $990B). Interactions row (Subtitle B) +95,448 outlays; KFF 82.4% share -> 78,656 (989,667-911,011).
- KFF by-year chart (https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/v0mna/10/): 2025 $0.045B, 2026 $17B, 2027 $46B, 2028 $61B, 2029 $92B, 2030 $111B, 2031 $128B, 2032 $139B, 2033 $151B, 2034 $165B (net of interactions, rounded).
- KFF Hawaii result confirmed from https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/36eMx/14/ : 3,902,000,000 (low 2,927M, high 4,878M), 15% of baseline; US 911,011,000,000.
- CBO Oct 28, 2025 supplemental: NOT FOUND. cbo.gov returns 403 to curl and search is blocked in browser; I could not verify whether it changes Medicaid outlays. KFF cites only the July 21 2025 estimate (cbo.gov/publication/61570).
- KFF provider-tax analysis page was updated after July 2025 (now 31 states, Nov 2025 survey). The July 23, 2025 archived version (https://web.archive.org/web/20250723005133/https://www.kff.org/policy-watch/which-states-might-have-to-reduce-provider-taxes-under-the-senate-reconciliation-bill/ ; chart https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/TXnv8/5/) lists 22 states, Hawaii "No affected hospital or MCO taxes". The newer 31-state per-state list could not be read (chart IDs 404 on dataset.csv); Hawaii has hospital and nursing-facility taxes only (FY2025 survey chart https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/0lk2G/33/), so unlikely to be in it.
- State-directed payment set for FY2024: KFF used its budget survey OR CMS preprint analysis; I could not read the survey list, so used the preprint analysis states with any SDP (37). Hawaii is "Likely affected" in the preprint analysis.
- Hawaii FY2024 FMAP 58.56% (KFF); I do not know how KFF applied the +1.5pt Q1 bump exactly (I used 0.375pt annualized).
