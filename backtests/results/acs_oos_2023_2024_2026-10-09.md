## As-of mode: what the ablation scripts actually consumed

The `compare_*_ablation.py` scripts (ML default-on, UI claims, national macro, natl_unemp, market, phi, Kalman) call `run_stratified_calibration` without an `as_of_mode` argument, so their fold residuals came from an **instant-mode** calibration (`effective_year ≤ anchor` only; no publication-date filter). Publication mode has existed since 2026-04-27 (`003e5d8`) but only the `refresh-data` workflow passes `--as-of-mode publication`.

On this panel the distinction is empty. The bundled panel holds 19638 1-year observations and **0 5-year observations** — the ML panel index also drops anything that is not `1y` (`ml_features.build_panel_index`). For a 1-year print of year Y, `effective_year = Y` and `publication_date = acs_1y_release_date(Y)`, which is exactly the as-of date publication mode uses for anchor Y, and the filter is `<=`. So the two truncations coincide: across 12960 (series, anchor) pairs at anchors 2014-2022 the instant and publication training sets differ in **0** cases, and in 0 of the 4260 OOS folds here.

Consequently no 5-year ACS observation whose window overlaps a target year could have been in any ablation's information set — there are none in the panel to leak. The ablation verdicts stand as publication-equivalent. The caveat that does apply to both modes: the auxiliary ML channels (BPS, SAIPE, LAUS, market, national macro, UI claims) are keyed by calendar year, not release date, so a lag-0 feature at anchor Y assumes the Y value was public by the Y 1-year release (September Y+1). Neither as-of mode checks that per source; it is a separate question from the 5-year overlap one and is not settled here.

## Verdict and what changed

**Consistent with the calibration at the pooled level.** Blended CI90 coverage
87.9% (band [85%, 95%]), pooled MAPE 7.1%, mean log bias +0.005. Point accuracy
is at or better than the best in-sample member for every indicator (largest
relative RMSE excess: B25071 +16%, inside the 20% tolerance). The strict
truth-masked re-run moves the pooled numbers by -0.3pp MAPE / -0.4pp coverage,
so the shipped payload is not being flattered by the 2023/2024 prints that sit
inside its long-horizon tuning folds.

**Five per-indicator coverage flags, not noise:**

* B25058, B25064, B25077 (rent, gross rent, home value) under-cover at
  80-84%, driven by the **xlarge** bucket (71-82%) while medium counties sit
  at 87-91%. Point errors are small (MAPE 4.6-6.1%); the intervals are too
  tight for the largest counties, whose sampling MOE is small and whose
  interval is therefore dominated by the forecast-variance term.
* pct_service_occupations 84.5% (0.5pp under), medium bucket 71.8%.
* S2301 (unemployment) over-covers at 99.2% — the in-sample evaluation
  already showed 98.9% / 100% for trend / ml, so this is known slack.

These are reasons to revisit the xlarge κ / conformal cells for the housing
indicators, not to reject the calibration.

**Hawaii-only (190 folds):** MAPE 7.0%, coverage 82.1%, bias +0.001. The
under-coverage is vacancy_rate (6/12) and pct_service_occupations (8/12); the
METHODOLOGY.md UI-claims section already records Hawaii coverage flags that
are pre-existing panel-wide κ/bias artefacts on 4-county cells.

**Action taken (this branch):** `run_acs_calibration --anchor-end` default and
the `refresh-data` workflow move from 2022 to 2023, so the next monthly
regeneration tunes on 2014-2021, takes its conformal quantiles from anchor
2022 (h=1,2 truth only) and evaluates on anchor 2023 (h=1 only). The
generator now tabulates truth availability per (anchor, h) in
`calibration.json["fold_truth_availability"]`, refuses an anchor with no
truth at any horizon, and raises if the conformal or evaluation anchor
yields no folds. **Known consequence:** with the calibration anchor at 2022
the `long` horizon bucket (h=3-5) gets no conformal records and falls back
to κ — the pre-conformal interval policy that had an under-coverage tail
(METHODOLOGY §8b). Under 2022 the long bucket was fed by anchor 2021 h=3
alone (one horizon, ~90 folds per cell), so the loss is of one thin cell;
but it is a loss, and the next regeneration's `evaluation_coverage` /
`calibration_horizons` should be read with that in mind. Reverting is a
one-line change in both places if the long-bucket κ fallback turns out
worse than the thin conformal cell it replaced.

Reproduce: `uv run python -m census_forecaster.scripts.evaluate_oos --masked`
(~7 min shipped pass, ~45 min for the masked regeneration on an M-series Mac).
